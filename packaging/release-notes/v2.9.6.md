## What's new in 2.9.6

**Fixed: the console side of a comparison clip could freeze for a few seconds.** When the recording encoder or the disk briefly fell behind, the recorder skipped whichever frame came next. The console side only moves on real console frames, and those arrive at a steady rhythm, so the skips could line up with them and hold the console side on one frame for seconds while the Glide side kept moving. Comparison clips now skip generated frames first and always keep one encoder slot free for the next real frame, so the console side keeps its true frame rate. Normal recordings are unchanged.

The recording log now also lists, per comparison clip, how many real and generated frames were recorded and whether any real frame was skipped.

**Tested how.** In CI on Windows:
- the build, unit tests, QML tests and the install test;
- a test of the new skip rule on WARP (Microsoft's software D3D12 device).

A simulation with the encoder only keeping up with two-thirds of the frames kept every real frame. Not yet tested with a capture card.

Also includes everything in 2.9.5:
- the raw console picture on the console side;
- styled badges.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new Glide downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
