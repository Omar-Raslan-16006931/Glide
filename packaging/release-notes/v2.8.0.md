## What's new in 2.8.0

Two pieces of Glide's own tech, made for console pictures.

**Frame Pacing Repair.** Many console games locked to 30 FPS don't deliver their frames evenly: some stay on
screen for 17 ms, others for 50 ms. The frame rate is right, but the motion judders, and frame generation keeps
the judder. Glide now puts every real frame back on an even grid before generating frames, so a 17/50 ms game
becomes 33/33. It only acts on uneven delivery of a locked frame rate, restarts after a stall, and leaves games
whose frame rate genuinely varies alone. A frame is shown at most one capture refresh later. Turn it on in the
quick setup; it shows how much of the picture it is re-timing.

**Live HUD Lock.** Frame generation drags still text, crosshairs and health bars along with the moving
background. Live HUD Lock finds those pixels on every frame, on your graphics card, and keeps the console's own
pixels there in the generated frames, with no boxes to draw. A pixel is released the moment it moves. Works with
DLSS, ANR, NVIDIA VFG, FSR and AMD FRC frame generation.

**Also:** the README, store art and promo graphics use the Glide colours and logo again; the Sunset City look
stays the app and website default for now.
