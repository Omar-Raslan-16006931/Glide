## What's new in 3.0.0

**Streaming mode.** A new page, opened with the broadcast button in the top bar, laid out like OBS Studio with Glide built in:
- **Preview** in the middle, showing the source, the game's frame rate and what Glide outputs.
- **Glide features** on the left: frame generation, adaptive multiplier, pacing repair, HUD lock, AI enhancement and lowest latency.
- **Controls** on the right: go live, record, save the instant replay, comparison clip, screenshot and your streaming platforms. While live, each platform shows its state and bitrate.
- **Audio mixer** along the bottom, like OBS's:
  - *Game sound* and *Microphone* each have a fader, a level meter and mute. These change what recordings, the instant replay and streams take.
  - *Monitor* changes only what you hear.
  - You choose whether the microphone goes into recordings, streams or both.
- **Microphone filters** (the sliders button on the microphone): rumble filter, 5-band equaliser with voice presets, noise gate and compressor, then a -1 dB limiter that is always on. The filters run once on the microphone, so every recording and stream gets the same voice.
- **Status bar** along the bottom: live time, bitrate and dropped frames, recording time, processing latency and the encoder.

**Vertical comparison clips for TikTok, Shorts and Reels.** The comparison clip has a third layout, *Vertical*:
- a 1080×1920 video, with the console on top and Glide below;
- each half shows the middle of the picture and fills half the screen;
- the frame-rate badges sit next to the divider, where those apps leave the screen free;
- H.264, ready to upload from your phone (HDR recordings stay HEVC 10-bit).

**Lowest latency in one tap.** A new switch in the quick setup turns on every latency setting that costs no picture quality:
- the graphics card queues only one frame (NVIDIA Low Latency on NVIDIA cards);
- fullscreen when the capture card or stream connects, skipping the desktop compositor;
- variable refresh sync on G-SYNC/FreeSync screens.

Frame generation, AI enhancement, HUD lock and pacing repair stay as they are. Turning the switch off brings back your previous settings.

**Setup guide.** A new section on the website, and **Glide-Setup-Guide.txt** attached to this release:
- the Glide settings to start from, and how to tune them;
- the settings for PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, Switch and Switch 2;
- the Windows settings;
- when to use HDR for playing, recording and streaming.

**Also new:**
- The startup music is off by default. Turn it on under *Settings, Appearance*.
- PS5 Remote Play uses H.265 by default for new pairings: a sharper picture at the same bitrate.

**Tested how.** In CI on Windows:
- the build;
- the unit tests, including the new microphone filter and mixer tests;
- the GPU tests on WARP, including the vertical clip layout in SDR and HDR;
- the QML tests for the streaming mode, mixer, filters, vertical layout and lowest latency;
- the install test, which opens the new page.

Not yet tested on real hardware with a capture card, microphone or live stream: reports are welcome.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new Glide downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
