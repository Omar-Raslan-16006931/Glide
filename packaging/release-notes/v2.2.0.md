## What's new in 2.2.0

**Optimise for my PC.** One click while your game is on screen: Glide tests every frame generation method,
AI enhancement and upscaler on your real picture, measures frame rate, 1% low and latency, confirms the
winner and applies it (Undo puts your settings back). It also detects whether the game runs at 30, 40 or
60 FPS.

**Game presets.** GTA VI and layouts for open-world, action, racing and shooter games. The minimap and HUD
are kept as the console's own pixels and never interpolated, so they don't smear. Save your own and share
them as a `PUSHFPS1:` code or a `.pushfps` file.

**Latency on screen.** Turn on *Show latency on screen* for the measured PC latency, frame rate and 1% low
over the picture.

**Share clips to Discord.** After a recording or instant replay is saved: one click copies the clip and opens
Discord, or posts it straight to your channel with a Discord webhook (up to 10 MB).

**Tested capture cards.** The quick setup shows whether your capture card has been tested, with a report
form for yours.

**OBS plugin 1.1.0.** Installs in the plugin layout OBS 33 uses and OBS 34 will require, so OBS's Plugin
Manager no longer flags it as Legacy. Tested in the latest OBS Studio.

**ANR frame generation by Apex Compute, one click to install.** [Apex Neural Rendering](https://github.com/apex-compute/ANR)
supports GeForce GTX 16 and newer, Radeon RX 5000 and newer and Intel Arc (Apex's list). Download its package from
Apex, then *Settings → Components and licenses → Install from download…*; Glide never ships ANR files. About
now links to Veyra and to ANR.

**14 languages.** Added Dutch, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Polish,
Russian and Arabic to English, Chinese and Japanese (Settings → Language). Buttons, menus, settings and
all Glide feature texts are translated by hand; longer help texts in the advanced pages are
machine-translated. Corrections from native speakers are welcome. Arabic text is shown correctly, the
layout is not mirrored yet.

**Light and black modes.** Settings → Appearance: the default dark grey, an almost-black mode for OLED
screens, or a light mode, all with readable text and controls.

**Also in this release** (since 2.1.8): Veyra 2.1.6 (instant replay, microphone recording, *exclude all
effects* regions, ANR frame generation offered as Veyra does), NVIDIA Image Scaling on any DirectX 12
graphics card, and the picture & sound editor.

**Tested:** built and tested by GitHub Actions on Windows Server without a graphics card (unit tests, the
interface tests, NVIDIA Image Scaling on Microsoft's WARP software renderer, a full install and start of the
app, the OBS plugin loaded into OBS Studio). Not yet tested by us with a capture card and a real graphics card
in this exact build; please report anything that looks wrong.
