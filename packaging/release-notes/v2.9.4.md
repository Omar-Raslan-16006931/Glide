## What's new in 2.9.4

**Comparison clip settings are easier to find.** The *Comparison clip* tile now opens its dialog even when nothing is playing, so you can choose the layout (split or side by side) and the length (10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds) before you start the capture card. The same two choices are also under *Settings, Playback*, next to the other recording settings. Recording itself still needs a picture on screen.

Also includes everything in 2.9.3:
- comparison clips in two layouts, with frame-rate labels on every clip (HDR included);
- recordings that open in Vegas Pro and other editors;
- sharper standard recordings.

**Tested how.** QML tests and the install test, in CI on Windows. No engine code changed since 2.9.3.
