## What's new in 2.7.0

**Adaptive multiplier.** Console games rarely hold one frame rate: a 30 FPS game dips to 25, a 40 FPS mode
wanders between 35 and 45, Elden Ring on Switch 2 sits anywhere from 30 to 40. Turn on *Adaptive multiplier* in
the quick setup and the multiplier becomes a ceiling: Glide measures the game's frame rate from the capture
card picture and, for every pair of real frames, uses the multiplier whose output lands closest to your target.
At 120 FPS: 30 FPS → 4X, 40 FPS → 3X, 36 FPS → 3X, 33 FPS → 4X. A change waits half a second so it never flickers,
and the quick setup shows what it is doing ("Now 3×: game 38 FPS → about 114 FPS"). It is the same idea as
NVIDIA's DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation, based on the console's frame rate, and it works with DLSS, ANR
and NVIDIA VFG frame generation.

**Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 presets.** 28 new game presets for games that run at 30 FPS: Switch games that
stay at 30 FPS on Switch 2 (Age of Calamity, Pokémon Legends: Arceus, Breath of the Wild, Xenoblade Chronicles X,
Monster Hunter Rise, The Witcher 3 and more) and Switch 2 ports (Cyberpunk 2077, Hogwarts Legacy, Final Fantasy VII
Remake and Rebirth, Star Wars Outlaws, Elden Ring and more). The list filters by console, one click sets the
console, frame rate and target, and games that dip get the adaptive multiplier. *Nintendo Switch* is now in the
console list too.

**Fix: 120 FPS targets with a 30 FPS game.** With an output cap of 120 FPS the engine worked out the multiplier
from the capture card's 60 Hz signal instead of the game's 30 FPS, and could run 2X (60 FPS) instead of 4X.
It now uses the measured game frame rate.
