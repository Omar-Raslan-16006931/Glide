## Glide 1.0.0

The first Glide release: a simpler, plug and play fork of PushFPS.

- **One screen.** Plug in the capture card, pick how many FPS you want, press Start.
- **Any FPS target.** Slide from 30 to 360, tap 60 / 120 / your screen's rate, or type any number up to 500.
- **No format menus.** The capture card's best mode (60 fps, 1080p, the fastest pixel format) and its own sound are picked for you.
- **Right frame generation for your graphics card.** NVIDIA DLSS frame generation on RTX 40/50; RTX 20/30 and other cards start on AMD FSR 3.1, which works on them.
- **Low lag mode** for competitive games, one switch.
- **One-click install** with no questions, plus `Glide.cmd` to download and run Glide from one file.

Everything from PushFPS is still there under **All settings**.

Glide is a fork of PushFPS by DosukaSOL, built on Veyra by Likely7 (GPL-3.0).
