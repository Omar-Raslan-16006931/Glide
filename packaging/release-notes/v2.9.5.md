## What's new in 2.9.5

**The console side of a comparison clip is now the raw console picture.** Before, AI picture enhancement and upscaling were applied to both sides, so only frame generation differed. Now the console side shows the picture exactly as the console sends it: no AI enhancement, no upscaling, no colour changes, only scaled to the clip's size. The Glide side shows everything Glide adds: frame generation, AI enhancement and upscaling. This works in both layouts (split and side by side), in SDR and HDR.

**Styled badges.** The plain text labels are replaced by badges: a dark rounded badge with *Console* and its frame rate on the console side, and the Glide logo with its frame rate (outlined in Glide green) on the other side. They are blended onto the video with soft edges.

**Tested how.** In CI on Windows:
- the build, unit tests, QML tests and the install test;
- GPU tests on WARP (Microsoft's software D3D12 device) for the raw console side in both layouts, SDR and HDR, and for the badge blending in SDR and HDR.

The badge drawing itself (fonts, logo) is only checked when the app runs. Not yet tested with a capture card: reports are welcome.

Also includes everything in 2.9.3 and 2.9.4:
- comparison clip layouts and length;
- recordings that open in Vegas Pro;
- sharper standard recordings.
