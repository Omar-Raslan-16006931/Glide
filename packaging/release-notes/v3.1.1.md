## What's new in 3.1.1

**Cleaner frame generation for 30 FPS games.** A 30 FPS game inside a 60 FPS capture repeats every picture (AABBCC). Before, Glide only removed those repeats when Frame Pacing Repair or the Adaptive multiplier was on, so with both off it generated frames between two identical pictures, which smears. Now picking a target in the quick setup always turns on automatic duplicate removal first.
- **Optimise for my PC** also tests every method this way, and Undo puts your previous setting back.
- If you chose 60→30 yourself in Pro mode, it stays.

**Clearer 120 Hz tip.** The quick setup's tip now says to use 120 Hz output only when your capture card *captures* 120 Hz, not just passes it through to your TV. It no longer lists the Elgato HD60 X, which captures at most 60 FPS. It's in all 13 languages.

**Streaming mode.** The platform and folder buttons sit on their own rows, so their labels are no longer cut off.

**Setup guide, updated** (attached as **Glide-Setup-Guide.txt**, and on the website at https://dosukasol.github.io/Glide/#guide):
- **Which target for your monitor:** 60, 75/100, 120, 144/165, 180, 240 and 360 Hz, for 30 and 60 FPS games, with the multiplier each needs and which frame generation methods can do it.
- **60 or 120 Hz on the console:** it depends on your capture card, not on your console (PS5 Pro included).
- **Capture card settings,** with thanks to Likely7, Veyra's developer, for his tips and measurements, checked against the makers' specs:
  - capture at 60 FPS, even for 30 FPS games;
  - uncompressed NV12 or P010 rather than MJPEG;
  - delay per capture format;
  - which card to buy: 5 Gbps USB, 10 Gbps USB or PCIe.
- **NVIDIA Video Frame Generation, Medium or High:** High fits 2× but is too heavy for 4× on most cards. Medium is the start point.

**Tested how.** In CI on Windows:
- the build and the unit tests;
- the GPU tests on WARP;
- the QML tests, including a new test for duplicate removal in the quick setup;
- the install test, which opens every main page.

Not yet tested on real hardware with a capture card: reports are welcome.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new Glide downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
