## What's new in 2.3.0

**Automatic HUD detection.** Open **HUD protection** in the quick setup while your game is playing and click
**Detect automatically**, then turn the camera or walk around for about five seconds. Glide finds the parts
of the picture that keep their detail while everything else moves (minimap, health, ammo) and draws up to four
boxes around them on a frame of your own game. Those areas keep the console's own pixels and are never
interpolated, so they don't smear, in any game.

**Correct the boxes by hand.** If detection is off, drag a box to move it, drag its corners to resize it, drag
on an empty spot to draw a new one, or delete it, then **Apply**. Game presets have an **Adjust HUD areas**
button that opens the same editor.

**Before/after comparison clips.** **Comparison clip** records a 15-second split-screen video: the left half
moves at the console's own frame rate, the right half shows every frame Glide generates, both labelled with
their frame rate. Share it to Discord from the card that appears afterwards.

**Help marks.** A small **?** beside settings that need explaining (game frame rate, target frame rate, AI
enhancement, low latency, variable refresh, output cap, encoding settings, NIS sharpness): hover it, click it
or tab to it for a short summary.

**New Glide title.** The home screen and About show the Glide wordmark as in the logo, with *FPS* in
green.

**Advanced recording settings** (from the previous build): rate control, quality level, encoder preset,
keyframe interval, audio bitrate and MP4 or MKV under *Settings → Playback*, off by default.

**Tested:** built and tested by GitHub Actions on Windows Server without a graphics card: unit tests including
the HUD detector, the comparison clip composition and HUD frame sampling on Microsoft's WARP software
renderer with the D3D12 debug layer, the interface tests, and a full install and start of the app. Not yet
tested by us with a capture card and a real graphics card in this exact build; please report anything that
looks wrong.
