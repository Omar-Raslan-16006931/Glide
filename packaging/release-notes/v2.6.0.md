## What's new in 2.6.0

**Sunset City.** Glide now opens with its own three-second intro, cut to the beat of the startup music: a
neon road rushes out of the dark, the two chevrons and the Glide logo land on the beats with a trail of
in-between frames, the sky turns to sunset over a palm-lined city while the counter climbs from 30 to 120 FPS,
and the logo glides into place on the home page. There it stays, filled with the sunset city.

- Click or press any key to skip the intro.
- *Settings → Appearance* has three separate switches: the Sunset City logo, the startup animation and the
  startup music. Turn any of them off; the classic logo comes back when the Sunset City logo is off.
- All of the art is drawn by Glide itself. The music is the startup track that comes with Veyra's runtime
  package; Glide plays it from there and does not include it.

**New website look.** https://dosukasol.github.io/Glide/ has a Sunset City look with an animated neon sky,
city and road, plus the original dark look and a new light look. Switch between them at the top of the page;
your choice is remembered.

**Credits.** Glide is built on [Veyra](https://github.com/Likely7/Veyra-NRVideo) by Likely7. ANR frame
generation is by [Apex Compute](https://apexcompute.com).
