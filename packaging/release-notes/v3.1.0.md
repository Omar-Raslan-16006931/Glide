## What's new in 3.1: the day-one GTA VI build

**Video playback mode.** A new mode (the play button in the top bar) for any video on your PC:
- pick a **window** (YouTube in your browser, a video app), a **whole screen** or a **video file**;
- switch on **Smoother** (frame generation: 24 or 30 FPS video looks like 60 or 120 FPS), **Sharper**
  (upscaling to your screen), **AI picture enhancement** (removes compression blocks and noise) and
  **SDR to HDR** (on an HDR monitor);
- press F11 to watch fullscreen.

**Scenes in streaming mode**, like OBS's, switched with one click or Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9:
- **Game scenes:** the picture, with your logo or a text tag (for example your channel name) in a corner.
- **Card scenes:** *Starting soon*, *Be right back*, *Thanks for watching*, or your own, with a background picture. They replace the game in recordings and streams and mute the game sound.
- Scenes change only what recordings and streams show; you keep seeing the game.

**Everything from 3.0** is in this build too: streaming mode with the audio mixer and microphone filters, vertical comparison clips for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, one-tap lowest latency, the startup music off by default, H.265 for new PS5 Remote Play pairings, and the setup guide.

**Setup guide.** **Glide-Setup-Guide.txt** is attached to this release, and the same guide is on the website (https://dosukasol.github.io/Glide/#guide). It covers:
- the settings to start from;
- the console settings for PS5, Xbox and Switch;
- the Windows settings;
- when to use HDR.

**Tested how.** In CI on Windows:
- the build and the unit tests;
- the GPU tests on WARP, including the scene overlays and cards in SDR and HDR;
- the QML tests for video playback mode, scenes, streaming mode and the rest of the quick setup;
- the install test, which opens every main page, including the two new modes.

Not yet tested on real hardware with a capture card or a live stream: reports are welcome.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new Glide downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
